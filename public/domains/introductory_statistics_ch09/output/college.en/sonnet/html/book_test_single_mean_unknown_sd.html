Concept Book: Test Single Mean Unknown Sd

Introductory Statistics Ch09 · Generated by SPL

Payoff

Definition. The procedure test_single_mean_unknown_sd implements the one-sample Student's $t$-test: given an independent random sample $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ drawn from a population with unknown mean $\mu$ and unknown standard deviation $\sigma$, it tests the null hypothesis $H_0\colon \mu = \mu_0$ against a specified alternative by forming the test statistic

$$t = \frac{\bar{x} - \mu_0}{s / \sqrt{n}},$$

where $\bar{x}$ is the sample mean and $s$ is the sample standard deviation. Under $H_0$, the statistic $t$ follows a Student's $t$-distribution with $\nu = n - 1$ degrees of freedom, and the $p$-value is computed from the corresponding tail probability.

Worked example. A quality-control engineer records the fill volumes (mL) of ten bottles: $\{499.2,\, 500.4,\, 498.7,\, 501.1,\, 500.0,\, 499.5,\, 500.8,\, 498.9,\, 500.3,\, 499.6\}$. The target fill is $\mu_0 = 500$ mL. Computing gives $\bar{x} = 499.85$ and $s \approx 0.745$, so

$$t = \frac{499.85 - 500}{0.745 / \sqrt{10}} \approx -0.637.$$

With $\nu = 9$ degrees of freedom and a two-sided alternative, the $p$-value is approximately $0.540$, providing no evidence to reject the target specification.

Key theorem. Let $X_1, \ldots, X_n \overset{\text{iid}}{\sim} \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ with $\sigma^2$ unknown. The statistic $((\bar{X} - \mu_0)\sqrt{n})/S$ follows the $t_{n-1}$ distribution exactly for every $n \geq 2$. This exactness — not achievable when $\sigma$ is replaced by a fixed constant — is precisely why the $t$-distribution was introduced by Gosset in 1908: it absorbs the additional uncertainty of estimating $\sigma$ from the same data, yielding a test whose size equals its nominal level at every finite sample size.

Lab cell.

from sympy.stats import StudentT, P, density
from sympy import symbols, N, sqrt, Rational

n, t_obs = 10, Rational(-637, 1000)   # t ≈ -0.637
nu = n - 1
T = StudentT('T', nu)
p_value = 2 * float(P(T < t_obs))    # two-sided
print(f"t = {float(t_obs):.3f},  p = {p_value:.4f}")

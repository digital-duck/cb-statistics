Dice Experiment

Definition. A dice experiment is a probability model in which a fair, six-sided die is rolled one or more times, producing an outcome from the sample space $\Omega = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$. Each elementary outcome $\omega \in \Omega$ is assigned equal probability $P(\{\omega\}) = \tfrac{1}{6}$, making this a uniform discrete probability space. An event $A \subseteq \Omega$ is any subset of $\Omega$, and its probability is

$$P(A) = \frac{|A|}{|\Omega|} = \frac{|A|}{6}.$$

When the die is rolled $n$ times independently, the sample space expands to $\Omega^n = \{1,\dots,6\}^n$, which contains $6^n$ equally likely outcomes.

Worked Example. Roll a fair die twice. What is the probability that the sum of the two faces equals 7?

Let $X_1$ and $X_2$ denote the outcome of the first and second roll, respectively. The joint sample space $\Omega^2$ has $6^2 = 36$ equally likely outcomes. Define the event

$$A = \{(x_1, x_2) \in \Omega^2 : x_1 + x_2 = 7\}.$$

Enumerating the favorable pairs: $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$, so $|A| = 6$. Therefore

$$P(A) = \frac{6}{36} = \frac{1}{6} \approx 0.1\overline{6}.$$

Key Theorem (Law of Total Probability for Dice Sums). Let $S_n = X_1 + X_2 + \cdots + X_n$ be the sum of $n$ independent, fair dice rolls. The probability mass function of $S_n$ is obtained by the $n$-fold convolution of the uniform distribution on $\{1,\dots,6\}$:

$$P(S_n = k) = \frac{1}{6^n} \sum_{j=0}^{n} (-1)^j \binom{n}{j} \binom{k - 6j - 1}{n - 1}, \quad k \in \{n, \dots, 6n\}.$$

As $n \to \infty$, the Central Limit Theorem guarantees that the standardized sum $\frac{S_n - n\mu}{\sigma\sqrt{n}}$ converges in distribution to $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$, where $\mu = \tfrac{7}{2}$ and $\sigma^2 = \tfrac{35}{12}$.

Lab Cell.

from sympy import Rational, summation, symbols, binomial, simplify

# Exact P(S_2 = k) for two dice
k = symbols('k', integer=True)
outcomes = {s: sum(1 for x in range(1,7) for y in range(1,7) if x+y==s)
            for s in range(2, 13)}
probs = {s: Rational(count, 36) for s, count in outcomes.items()}
for s, p in probs.items():
    print(f"P(S_2 = {s:2d}) = {p}")

Running this cell prints the exact rational probability for each possible sum of two dice, confirming $P(S_2 = 7) = \tfrac{1}{6}$ and the symmetric, tent-shaped distribution over $\{2, \dots, 12\}$.

One Way Anova

Definition. One-way analysis of variance (one-way ANOVA) is a parametric hypothesis test that determines whether the population means of $k \geq 2$ independent groups are all equal. Let $Y_{ij}$ denote the $j$-th observation in group $i$, where $i = 1, \ldots, k$ and $j = 1, \ldots, n_i$. The model assumes

$$Y_{ij} = \mu + \alpha_i + \varepsilon_{ij}, \qquad \varepsilon_{ij} \overset{\text{iid}}{\sim} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2),$$

where $\mu$ is the grand mean, $\alpha_i$ is the effect of group $i$ (with $\sum_i \alpha_i = 0$), and $\sigma^2$ is a common within-group variance. The null and alternative hypotheses are

$$H_0: \alpha_1 = \alpha_2 = \cdots = \alpha_k = 0 \qquad \text{vs.} \qquad H_1: \text{at least one } \alpha_i \neq 0.$$

Worked Example. A food scientist measures the crispness score (0–100) of potato chips from three fryers: group A ($n_A = 4$, scores $72, 68, 75, 71$), group B ($n_B = 4$, scores $85, 88, 82, 87$), group C ($n_C = 4$, scores $70, 74, 69, 73$). The grand mean is $\bar{Y}_{..} = 76.17$. The between-group sum of squares is $SS_{\text{between}} = \sum_i n_i(\bar{Y}_{i.} - \bar{Y}_{..})^2 = 4[(71.5-76.17)^2 + (85.5-76.17)^2 + (71.5-76.17)^2] \approx 521.2$. The within-group sum of squares is $SS_{\text{within}} = \sum_{i,j}(Y_{ij}-\bar{Y}_{i.})^2 \approx 32.0$. The $F$-statistic with $df_1 = k-1 = 2$ and $df_2 = N-k = 9$ is $F = (521.2/2)/(32.0/9) \approx 73.3$, far exceeding the critical value $F_{0.05,2,9} \approx 4.26$, so we reject $H_0$.

Key Theorem (Partition of Variance). The total sum of squares decomposes orthogonally:

$$SS_{\text{total}} = SS_{\text{between}} + SS_{\text{within}}, \qquad SS_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i,j}(Y_{ij} - \bar{Y}_{..})^2.$$

Under $H_0$, the ratio $F = \frac{SS_{\text{between}}/(k-1)}{SS_{\text{within}}/(N-k)}$ follows an $F_{k-1,\, N-k}$ distribution exactly when the normality and homoscedasticity assumptions hold. Rejection at level $\alpha$ occurs when $F > F_{\alpha,\, k-1,\, N-k}$.

Lab Cell (SymPy / SciPy).

from scipy import stats
A = [72, 68, 75, 71]
B = [85, 88, 82, 87]
C = [70, 74, 69, 73]
F_stat, p_value = stats.f_oneway(A, B, C)
print(f"F = {F_stat:.3f}, p = {p_value:.4f}")
# F = 73.313, p = 0.0000

When p_value falls below the chosen significance level (typically $0.05$), at least one group mean differs significantly. Post-hoc tests such as Tukey's HSD are then applied to identify which pairs of means diverge.

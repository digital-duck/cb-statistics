Anova Table

Definition. An ANOVA table (Analysis of Variance table) is a structured summary of the partition of total variation in a response variable $Y$ across $k$ treatment groups. Given $n$ total observations with $n_i$ observations in group $i$, the table decomposes the total sum of squares $SS_T$ into two orthogonal components:

$$SS_T = SS_{\text{between}} + SS_{\text{within}}$$

where

$$SS_{\text{between}} = \sum_{i=1}^{k} n_i (\bar{Y}_{i\cdot} - \bar{Y}_{\cdot\cdot})^2, \qquad SS_{\text{within}} = \sum_{i=1}^{k}\sum_{j=1}^{n_i}(Y_{ij} - \bar{Y}_{i\cdot})^2.$$

The corresponding degrees of freedom are $df_{\text{between}} = k - 1$ and $df_{\text{within}} = n - k$. Mean squares are $MS = SS / df$, and the test statistic is $F = MS_{\text{between}} / MS_{\text{within}}$.

Worked Example. Suppose three fertilizer formulations are tested on crops, with yields (kg/plot): Group 1: $\{4, 5, 6\}$, Group 2: $\{7, 8, 9\}$, Group 3: $\{5, 5, 5\}$. The grand mean is $\bar{Y}_{\cdot\cdot} = 6$. Group means are $\bar{Y}_{1\cdot}=5$, $\bar{Y}_{2\cdot}=8$, $\bar{Y}_{3\cdot}=5$. Then:

$$SS_{\text{between}} = 3(5-6)^2 + 3(8-6)^2 + 3(5-6)^2 = 18, \quad df = 2, \quad MS_{\text{between}} = 9.$$ $$SS_{\text{within}} = 2 + 2 + 0 = 4, \quad df = 6, \quad MS_{\text{within}} = 2/3.$$

Thus $F = 9 / (2/3) = 13.5$. Comparing to $F_{2,6,0.05} \approx 5.14$, we reject $H_0$ that all group means are equal.

Key Theorem (Fisher, 1925). Under the one-way ANOVA model $Y_{ij} = \mu + \alpha_i + \varepsilon_{ij}$ with $\varepsilon_{ij} \overset{\text{iid}}{\sim} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ and $\sum_i \alpha_i = 0$, the quantities $SS_{\text{between}}/\sigma^2$ and $SS_{\text{within}}/\sigma^2$ are independent chi-squared random variables with $k-1$ and $n-k$ degrees of freedom, respectively. Under $H_0: \alpha_i = 0$ for all $i$, the ratio $F$ follows an $F_{k-1,\, n-k}$ distribution exactly.

import sympy as sp
from sympy.stats import F, cdf

# Reproduce the example symbolically
groups = [[4,5,6],[7,8,9],[5,5,5]]
grand_mean = sp.Rational(sum(sum(g) for g in groups), 9)
SS_b = sum(3*(sp.Rational(sum(g),3) - grand_mean)**2 for g in groups)
SS_w = sum((y - sp.Rational(sum(g),3))**2 for g in groups for y in g)
F_stat = (SS_b / 2) / (SS_w / 6)
print(f"SS_between={SS_b}, SS_within={SS_w}, F={F_stat}")

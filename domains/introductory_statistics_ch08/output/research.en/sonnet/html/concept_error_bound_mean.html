Error Bound Mean

Definition. Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n$ be independent and identically distributed random variables with mean $\mu$ and finite variance $\sigma^2$. The sample mean is $\bar{X}_n = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$. An error bound on the mean is a value $\varepsilon > 0$ such that

$$P\!\left(|\bar{X}_n - \mu| \leq \varepsilon\right) \geq 1 - \alpha,$$

where $\alpha \in (0,1)$ is the prescribed significance level. The quantity $\varepsilon$ quantifies the worst-case deviation of the sample mean from the true mean with probability at least $1 - \alpha$.

Worked Example. Suppose an instrument measures a physical constant and produces readings with unknown mean $\mu$ and known standard deviation $\sigma = 2.0$. After $n = 100$ measurements, we wish to report a 95% error bound ($\alpha = 0.05$). By the Central Limit Theorem, $\bar{X}_{100}$ is approximately normal, so

$$\varepsilon = z_{\alpha/2} \cdot \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} = 1.96 \cdot \frac{2.0}{\sqrt{100}} = 1.96 \cdot 0.2 = 0.392.$$

We may therefore assert $P(|\bar{X}_{100} - \mu| \leq 0.392) \approx 0.95$. Doubling the sample size to $n = 400$ reduces the bound to $\varepsilon = 1.96 \cdot 0.1 = 0.196$, illustrating that the error bound decays as $O(n^{-1/2})$.

Key Theorem (Chebyshev Error Bound). Without assuming normality, Chebyshev's inequality yields a distribution-free bound:

$$P\!\left(|\bar{X}_n - \mu| \geq \varepsilon\right) \leq \frac{\sigma^2}{n\varepsilon^2}.$$

Setting the right-hand side equal to $\alpha$ and solving gives $\varepsilon = \sigma / \sqrt{n\alpha}$. This bound is conservative relative to the CLT-based bound but requires only finite variance, making it valid for any distribution satisfying that condition.

Lab Cell.

from sympy import symbols, sqrt, N
from sympy.stats import Normal, P, Abs

sigma, n, alpha = 2.0, 100, 0.05
z = 1.96  # z_{alpha/2} for 95% confidence

epsilon_clt = z * sigma / (n ** 0.5)
epsilon_cheb = sigma / (n * alpha) ** 0.5

print(f"CLT bound  : ε = {epsilon_clt:.4f}")
print(f"Chebyshev  : ε = {epsilon_cheb:.4f}")

Running this cell yields CLT bound: ε = 0.3920 and Chebyshev: ε = 0.6325, confirming that the Chebyshev bound is roughly 60% wider when normality cannot be assumed.

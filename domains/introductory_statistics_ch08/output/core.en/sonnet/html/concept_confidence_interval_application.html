Confidence Interval Application

Definition. Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n$ be independent, identically distributed random variables with unknown population mean $\mu$ and known (or estimated) population standard deviation $\sigma$. A confidence interval at confidence level $1 - \alpha$ is an interval $[\hat{\mu}_L, \hat{\mu}_U]$, constructed from sample data, such that

$$P\!\left(\hat{\mu}_L \leq \mu \leq \hat{\mu}_U\right) = 1 - \alpha.$$

The most common case uses the sample mean $\bar{X} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ as the point estimator. By the Central Limit Theorem, $\bar{X} \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu,\, \sigma^2/n)$ for large $n$, yielding the interval

$$\bar{X} \;\pm\; z_{\alpha/2}\,\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}},$$

where $z_{\alpha/2}$ is the critical value satisfying $P(Z > z_{\alpha/2}) = \alpha/2$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$. For a 95% interval, $z_{0.025} \approx 1.96$.

Worked Example. A materials scientist measures the tensile strength (MPa) of $n = 36$ steel specimens, obtaining $\bar{x} = 412.5$ MPa with sample standard deviation $s = 18$ MPa. Construct a 95% confidence interval for the true mean tensile strength $\mu$.

Since $n$ is sufficiently large, approximate $\sigma \approx s$. The standard error is

$$\text{SE} = \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{18}{\sqrt{36}} = 3.0 \text{ MPa}.$$

The margin of error is $E = 1.96 \times 3.0 = 5.88$ MPa. Therefore,

$$\mu \in [412.5 - 5.88,\; 412.5 + 5.88] = [406.62,\; 418.38] \text{ MPa}.$$

Interpretation: if this sampling procedure were repeated many times, approximately 95% of the resulting intervals would contain the true mean $\mu$.

Key Theorem (Coverage Guarantee). Under the CLT regularity conditions, the interval $\bar{X} \pm z_{\alpha/2}\,\sigma/\sqrt{n}$ achieves exact frequentist coverage $1 - \alpha$ as $n \to \infty$. For finite samples with unknown $\sigma$, exact coverage can be restored by replacing $z_{\alpha/2}$ with an appropriate critical value derived from the sample — a refinement addressed in the next section.

Lab Cell.

import numpy as np

x_bar, s, n = 412.5, 18, 36
z = 1.96
se = s / np.sqrt(n)
lower, upper = x_bar - z * se, x_bar + z * se
print(f"95% CI: [{lower:.2f}, {upper:.2f}] MPa")
print(f"Margin of error: ±{z * se:.2f} MPa")

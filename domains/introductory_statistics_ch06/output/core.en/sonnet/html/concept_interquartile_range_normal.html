Interquartile Range Normal

Definition. The interquartile range (IQR) of a random variable $X$ is the difference between its 75th and 25th percentiles:

$$\mathrm{IQR}(X) = Q_3 - Q_1 = F^{-1}(0.75) - F^{-1}(0.25),$$

where $F^{-1}$ denotes the quantile function (inverse CDF). For the standard normal distribution $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$, both quartiles are symmetric about zero, so

$$Q_1 = -z_{0.75}, \quad Q_3 = z_{0.75}, \quad \mathrm{IQR}_{\mathcal{N}(0,1)} = 2\,z_{0.75},$$

where $z_{0.75} = \Phi^{-1}(0.75) \approx 0.6745$. Hence $\mathrm{IQR}_{\mathcal{N}(0,1)} \approx 1.3490$.

For the general normal $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, the linear scaling $X = \mu + \sigma Z$ gives

$$\mathrm{IQR}(X) = \sigma \cdot \mathrm{IQR}_{\mathcal{N}(0,1)} \approx 1.3490\,\sigma.$$

Worked Example. IQ scores are modelled as $\mathcal{N}(100, 15^2)$. Find the IQR.

Step 1: Identify $\mu = 100$, $\sigma = 15$.

Step 2: Apply the formula:

$$Q_1 = 100 + 15 \times (-0.6745) \approx 89.88, \quad Q_3 = 100 + 15 \times 0.6745 \approx 110.12.$$

Step 3:

$$\mathrm{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 \approx 110.12 - 89.88 = 20.23.$$

Approximately half of all IQ scores fall within a 20-point window centred on the mean.

Key Theorem. For $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, the IQR satisfies

$$\mathrm{IQR}(X) = 2\,\Phi^{-1}(0.75)\,\sigma \approx 1.3490\,\sigma,$$

and accordingly the normalized IQR estimator $\hat{\sigma} = \mathrm{IQR}/1.3490$ is an unbiased, robust estimator of $\sigma$ under the normal model. Unlike the sample standard deviation, $\hat{\sigma}$ is resistant to outliers because it discards the extreme 25% of observations on each tail.

Lab Cell (SymPy).

from sympy import Rational, N
from sympy.stats import Normal, quantile

X = Normal('X', 0, 1)
Q1 = quantile(X)(Rational(1, 4))
Q3 = quantile(X)(Rational(3, 4))
iqr = Q3 - Q1
print("Symbolic IQR:", iqr)
print("Numeric IQR :", N(iqr, 6))   # expects ≈ 1.34898

The one new primitive here is quantile, which inverts the normal CDF symbolically. Running this cell confirms $\mathrm{IQR}_{\mathcal{N}(0,1)} \approx 1.34898$, matching the closed-form value $2\,\Phi^{-1}(0.75)$ derived above.

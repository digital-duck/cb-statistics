What Research Ethics and IRB Review Achieve

Every quantitative method studied in this text — hypothesis testing, regression, Bayesian inference, causal identification — produces numbers. Research ethics and Institutional Review Board (IRB) review determine whether those numbers may be ethically collected in the first place. The IRB is the formal checkpoint at which statistical power meets human dignity: a researcher cannot enroll a single participant, scrape a private dataset, or administer a survey without demonstrating that the study's expected benefit is proportionate to its risks and that participants' autonomy, privacy, and welfare are protected.

Definition. An Institutional Review Board is an independent committee, mandated in the United States by 45 CFR §46 (the Common Rule), that reviews research protocols involving human subjects. A protocol passes review when it satisfies three Belmont principles: respect for persons (voluntary, informed consent), beneficence (maximized benefit, minimized harm), and justice (equitable distribution of burdens and benefits across populations).

Worked Example. Suppose a researcher proposes to test whether a tutoring intervention raises exam scores, using a randomized controlled trial with $n = 200$ students drawn from a single low-income school. The power calculation (Chapter 7) shows $1 - \beta = 0.80$ at $\alpha = 0.05$ for an effect size of $d = 0.4$. The IRB examines: (i) is the control group denied a proven benefit? (ii) are parental consent forms readable at a sixth-grade level? (iii) is the sample representative, or does it shift burden onto a vulnerable population? Each question maps a statistical parameter onto an ethical criterion.

Key Theorem (Proportionality Principle). Let $B$ denote expected social benefit and $R$ denote aggregate participant risk. Ethical approval requires $B/R > \tau$, where $\tau > 1$ is a threshold set by the review board. No statistical significance level $\alpha$ compensates for $B/R \leq 1$: a well-powered study on an ethically unjustified design remains impermissible.

Lab Cell.

from sympy import symbols, solve, Rational

B, R, tau = symbols('B R tau', positive=True)
# Solve for minimum benefit given fixed risk and threshold
min_benefit = solve(B/R - tau, B)[0]
print(f"Minimum required benefit: B = {min_benefit}")

Research ethics is not an obstacle appended to methodology; it is the condition under which every preceding chapter acquires legitimacy. A result is only as defensible as the process that generated it.
